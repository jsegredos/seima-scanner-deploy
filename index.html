User Guide

🏠 Getting Started

The Seima Product Selector helps you build and share product selections from the Seima bathroom and kitchen range.

  • Select Products - Browse and search to find products
  • View Selection - See all products you've added
  • Clear Selection - Remove all products and start fresh

🔍 Finding Products

Tap Select Products to browse the catalogue. You can:

  • Search by product name (e.g., "basin", "toilet", "mixer")
  • Search by product code (e.g., "123456")
  • Scan barcodes in Seima showrooms

📷 Scanning Barcodes

In a Seima showroom? Tap the Scan button to use your camera:

  • Allow camera access when prompted
  • Point at the product barcode
  • Hold steady until recognised

Tip: Ensure good lighting and hold 10-15cm from the barcode.

➕ Adding Products

When viewing a product:

  1. Adjust the quantity if needed
  2. Select a room to organise your selection
  3. Add any notes about this product
  4. Tap Add to Selection

📋 Viewing Your Selection

Tap View Selection to see all products. You can:

  • Change quantities
  • Move products between rooms
  • Edit notes
  • Remove products

📧 Sending Your Selection

Ready to share? From the selection screen:

  1. Tap Send Email
  2. Enter your name, email, and project details
  3. Choose options (hide pricing, include spreadsheet)
  4. Tap Send

You'll receive a professional PDF presentation by email.

⚙️ Settings

Tap the gear icon (⚙️) to enter your contact details. These appear on your PDF presentation.

💡 Tips

  • Your selection saves automatically in your browser
  • Send the email to yourself for a permanent copy
  • Add the app to your home screen for quick access
  • Use rooms to organise products by location
